# Five exact Stats NZ evidence gaps

This follow-up covers only the five previously selected national cells. It makes
no runtime change, credential request, new-source selection or release claim.
The prior coverage record and reader targets remain immutable.

The targets are comparison hypotheses, not certified numbers. Agriculture
coverage must match the agricultural production survey/census universe and
national total. Forest planting is a flow over the stated March year; apples
and sheep are June stocks. Projection cells are projections, not observed
employment, population or households; Medium B cannot stand in for Medium A.

Pass requires a dated permissible original official export with exact dimension
identity, period, unit, adjustment and revision/vintage metadata, independently
extracted without the Morning Rows reader, plus reconciliation with a separately
presented official table/publication wherever available. Exact comparisons use
zero tolerance at the publisher's recorded precision. Shared Stats NZ statistical
lineage will be stated. An export sharing the reader's underlying service may
verify extraction but cannot be described as independent statistical estimation.

A rounded official value can establish only the displayed precision. It cannot
recover extra digits, even when the target falls within its rounding interval.
A different release, scenario, geography, population or counting convention is
not accepted. Missing, suppressed, confidential or absent cells are never zero.
No approximate matching, regional summation or inferred dates are permitted.

Retain minimal licence-permitted aggregate specimens, original URLs, acquisition
timestamps, status and content hashes; distinguish HTTP body hashes from derived
excerpt hashes. Full transient bodies stay outside the repository. Record
falsifiable blockers if exact evidence is unavailable. No contacts, private
headers, credentials or session material are retained. A blocked result does not
complete the original numerical acceptance or future-vintage approval.
