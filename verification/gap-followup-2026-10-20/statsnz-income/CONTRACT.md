# Exact income and LEED evidence follow-up — 20 October 2026

This evidence-only follow-up revisits the five retained Stats NZ cells in the
accepted reader: INC_INC_001, INC_INC_011, LEED_AP1_001, LEED_AP1_002 and
LEED_Q1W_001. The original targets are comparison inputs, never the numerical
oracle. No runtime, secret, account, release or monitoring changes are authorized
within this evidence work. The LEED subdirectory has a separate extraction owner.

The first income target is INC_INC_001 at 202606, key
`.98.98.98.98.MED_WEEK_INC`: total ethnic groups, regions, sexes and ages,
median weekly earnings, NZD 1,391. The dated June 2026 headline independently
presents NZD 1,419 for wage/salary earnings. These values cannot be substituted
until the exact table's job coverage, earnings sources, population and vintage are
established. Main-job earnings versus all wage/salary earnings is a hypothesis to
check, not an accepted explanation.

The second income target is INC_INC_011 at 202606, key
`.98.98.MED_INC_ALL_SOUR`: total regions and household types, median weekly
income from all sources collected, NZD 2,438. The official household population
excludes households whose members are all outside ages 18–64. Its June-quarter
weekly snapshot is not an annual estimate. Household gross/net basis must be
established independently; a statement about individual earnings is insufficient.

Acceptance requires a separately extracted original official presentation or
permitted public export matching each exact dimension, population, geography,
period convention, unit, adjustment, missing-value semantics and value at the
same vintage. Compare integers exactly; a rounded headline cannot certify extra
digits. Newer data cannot certify the retained cells. Evidence sourced from the
same Stats NZ survey has shared statistical lineage even when the presentation
and extraction are independent of the reader.

Public HTML, metadata and official download/export links may be inspected and
minimal licence-permitted originals retained with URL, retrieval time, response
hash and stated publication/revision metadata. Do not inspect or extract the
Worker's key, register credentials, bypass access controls, contact publishers,
repeatedly retry an unavailable route, or treat a third-party graph as an official
oracle. A denial is a documented evidence boundary.

Controls must distinguish median from mean, main job from all jobs, wages from
all income, individual from household, population exclusions, quarter snapshot
from annual total, current versus older release, and missing/suppressed values
from zero. A semantic clarification may close a metadata sub-gap while an exact
numeric cell remains unresolved. Report each row's actual boundary; no all-series
or overall maintenance completion claim follows from this evidence work.
