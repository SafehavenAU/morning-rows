# Research contracts and acceptance criteria

Defined before writing the retrieval demonstrations. These are research prototypes, not production readers. The existing eight desks and runtime remain unchanged. The NZ rent contract and demonstration are reused from `../rent-demand-2026-10-17/`.

## UK, England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland: monthly claimant briefs

- **Question:** what is the latest published claimant count here, and how does it compare with the same month a year earlier? Test all five national scopes and Birmingham, Cardiff, Glasgow City and Belfast. These local examples extend an authored Birmingham agent workflow; they are constructed tests, not observed queries from each nation.
- **Source:** ONS Nomis `NM_162_1` / `UCJSA`, not JSA-only `NM_1_1`. Parameters `gender=0`, `age=0`, `measure=1`, `measures=20100`, `date=latestMINUS12-latest`. Country codes and names must match the publisher codelist; local-authority codes must match returned names. Wales/Scotland/NI can be typed as regions by the transport while still representing their national boundaries.
- **Measure/population:** administrative claimant count, all sexes, age 16+, JSA and relevant UC claimants under the publisher's benefit definition. Not an ILO unemployment estimate, claimant rate, total labour supply, or count of unique available recruits. Eligibility/policy and double-counting caveats apply; the NISRA notes describe some JSA/UC overlap and geographically unclassified records.
- **Period/unit/adjustment:** monthly reference-date stock, persons rounded to the nearest five; not seasonally adjusted. Current period August 2026, reference day 13 August, released 15 September. Next release 20 October, so unchanged values on 17 October are expected. Revisions normally affect the previous month; methodological revisions can reach further back. Preserve retrieval/release vintage.
- **Missingness:** require free-for-publication (`OBS_CONF=F`) and normal-value (`OBS_STATUS=A`) for demonstrated numeric answers. Reject any other flag, missing value or non-finite/negative count; never infer zero. An explicitly reported numeric zero is a rounded published zero, not proof that absolutely no people exist in the category.
- **Reuse:** ONS Crown copyright, Open Government Licence, publisher attribution. NISRA comparison under its Crown copyright/OGL terms.
- **Acceptance:** exactly 13 consecutive months for nine verified geographies, one observation per geography/month, fixed dimensions and labels, complete record count, all flags valid. UK versus four-nation sum may differ by at most 12.5 persons from five separately rounded cells; no forced reconciliation. Year-over-year difference uses the same definition/geography. Reconcile NI and Belfast against the NISRA LGD table; do not compare them as identical to the seasonally adjusted headline. Decline questions asking for unemployment or individual job availability.

## Australia: completed-day wholesale price briefs

- **Question:** how many five-minute prices were negative on the selected complete date, and what was the time-weighted mean? Test SA1 and VIC1 on 16 October 2026. This is a calendar day in the NEM's market clock, not the NEM trading-day convention or a moving 24-hour window ending now.
- **Source:** AEMO monthly `PRICE_AND_DEMAND_202610_{REGION}.csv`. Required original fields `REGION`, `SETTLEMENTDATE`, `RRP`, `PERIODTYPE=TRADE`; demand is not used. Compare selected cells with original NEMweb `DISPATCH/PRICE` records, matching region, settlement time and non-intervention run.
- **Measure/coverage:** regional wholesale reference price, AUD/MWh, nominal/unadjusted; not a retail tariff, bill, national mean or forecast. NEM covers NSW/ACT, Queensland, Victoria, South Australia and Tasmania; this demo samples two regions and makes no WA/NT claim.
- **Period:** five-minute interval-ending stamps, fixed market AEST/UTC+10. Exactly 288 intervals for the selected calendar day, from 00:05 through following 00:00. Never apply Melbourne/Adelaide daylight-saving offsets to source stamps. A full month can straddle files for a rolling window; this demo does not implement arbitrary windows.
- **Freshness/revisions:** the tested monthly file stops at 17 October 00:00 AEST. Do not describe it as the last 24 hours at research time or as live dispatch. Publisher dashboard dispatch updates every five minutes; the monthly-file update SLA and correction schedule are not established. File vintage is mutable, so retain retrieval hash and do not claim finality. Four selected original dispatch records say FIRM; that does not establish every interval's status.
- **Missingness:** zero and negative prices are valid. Blank, malformed, non-finite, duplicate, forecast or wrong-region rows fail the selected window; never treat missing intervals as zero or average a partial day.
- **Reuse:** AEMO copyright permissions allow use with accurate attribution; this is not claimed to be OGL or Creative Commons.
- **Acceptance:** 288 unique contiguous observations per selected region; negative count excludes explicit zeros; Decimal-based mean over equal-length intervals. Four comparison prices agree with separate dispatch CSVs within AUD0.005/MWh (monthly file rounds to cents). Clearly refuse a live/forecast/household-savings answer.

## Canada: dated currency conversion and recent history

- **Question:** what CAD reference amount corresponds to USD1,000 or GBP2,500 on a specified published date, and what changed over the observed week? This is a reference calculation, not an executable quote or tax ruling.
- **Source:** Bank of Canada Valet, series `FXUSDCAD` and `FXGBPCAD`, `start_date=2026-10-12`, `end_date=2026-10-16`. Fetch original JSON and compare with original CSV and the separately presented daily-rate table.
- **Measure/coverage:** indicative daily average, CAD per one unit of foreign currency. No seasonal/inflation adjustment. Use the exact publisher series definition; do not invert the quote silently. Currency coverage is per series, not a claim of every currency or national subregion.
- **Period/cadence:** business-day daily averages, normally published by 16:30 Eastern Time. 12 October is explicitly labelled a bank holiday by the official daily table; there are four observations, not five. Weekends/holidays do not get carried-forward values without a separately requested policy. Missing is not zero. No vintage-finality or immutable-history promise was established.
- **Reuse:** Bank of Canada terms permit reuse with attribution, accurate presentation and identification of transformations. Content is available free from the Bank; retain that notice if ever included in a paid product. No API key.
- **Acceptance:** unique dates within the requested range; correct unit metadata; both selected series present and strictly positive finite numeric values; fail missing cells/series. Match all eight JSON values with official CSV; independently extract USD/GBP rows from the official HTML table. Multiplication uses Decimal with cent rounding. Return `not_published_on_date` for the holiday, never Friday's/latest rate as Monday's rate. No unsupported geographic labels.
