# UK CPI repair contract

Scope: existing `price_index` sections on the UK and England desks. Both explicitly represent **United Kingdom** data. No new source or England-only CPI is introduced. The old UK section had one index; the unsafe two-series join was in England. Use a shared validated reader, preserving legacy `period`, `value`, `unit` and England's `year_change` types and adding explicit numeric fields and provenance.

Original endpoints: ONS `/economy/inflationandpriceindices/timeseries/d7bt/mm23/data` (all-items CPI index, 2015=100) and `d7g7/mm23/data` (published 12-month percentage change). Select the unique latest monthly row in each `months` array, verify it equals `description.date`, and require the **same month and publication metadata**. Annual or quarterly rows cannot determine selection. Never fall back to the previous month on a missing or inconsistent latest value.

Measure/population: ONS UK all-items consumer-price basket; not household-specific inflation or CPIH. Geography is United Kingdom, not England; no subnational boundary assertion. Observation period is calendar month; keep legacy `YYYY MON` and additive `reference_month: YYYY-MM`. Index unit 2015=100; annual rate percent, not percentage-point change. No transformation/seasonal adjustment is applied by Morning Rows; the retrieved metadata does not independently document seasonal-adjustment policy, so that limitation remains explicit. Confidence intervals are not supplied in these responses. Do not claim statistical finality or comparability with other price indices.

Publication cadence: monthly. Preserve publisher release timestamp as supplied and next-release text; do not fabricate an exact next-release time. This specimen is August 2026, released according to metadata `2026-09-15T23:00:00.000Z`, next-release date label 21 October 2026. Revisions must change content version; retrieval alone must not. Check later releases rather than freezing these dates in runtime code.

Licence: OGL v3; ONS attribution and original URLs retained. Zero annual inflation is valid; index must be positive. Blank, nonnumeric, missing and suppressed cells cannot become zero. This numeric headline task fails explicitly if its necessary selected cells are not numeric; no claim that absent cells are suppressed without publisher evidence.

Acceptance: exact CDIDs, MM23 dataset, titles/base, units, unique latest monthly coordinates; identical reference periods and release metadata; valid finite values; year-earlier index consistent with official annual rate within interval implied by one-decimal rounding; source failure remains isolated. At the retained vintage, index 143.6 and annual rate 3.1 agree with Table 1 and the bulletin narrative. The year-earlier index is 139.3. Different extractors/presentations share ONS statistical lineage. Retained subset: `test/fixtures/uk-cpi.json`, full-response hashes and attribution included.

Real Cloudflare live access, consumer compatibility and production checks are mandatory release gates. A workspace 200 response or a passing fixture is insufficient. Unsupported requests for England-only CPI, CPIH, adjusted figures, arbitrary history or a contractual interpretation remain unsupported.
